How it works

A PDF or Word resume is parsed by Claude into structured data. Saved searches fetch jobs every 1 to 48 hours, each feed and keyword in parallel. Tailoring produces a resume, a cover letter, a fit score and matched and missing keywords, previewed before anything is saved. When you tailor for a new role, similar past roles are suggested for reuse, so "Engineer" and "Developer" titles match. Resumes print to A4 PDF from eight templates, and applications move through a ten-stage status pipeline.

Pipeline stages, in order:

  1. Parse resume
  2. Fetch jobs
  3. Tailor
  4. Preview and save
  5. Track

What it guards against

One job feed quietly shrinking the results. Each fetch reports new, duplicate and dropped jobs, with a separate error for each feed, so a failing feed shows up instead of just returning fewer jobs.

Stack

Python, FastAPI, Claude, React, TanStack Query, Tailwind, JWT auth with email verification and admin permissions.
